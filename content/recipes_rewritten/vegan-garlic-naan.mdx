## Ingredients
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1.5 Tbsp unsalted vegan butter
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 0.5 cup unsweetened soy yoghurt
- 0.25 cup unsweetened soy milk
- 1.5 Tbsp granulated sugar
- 0.5 cup fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. In a bowl, melt the vegan butter and mix in the minced 2 garlic cloves, minced. Set aside.
2. In a large bowl, combine the flour, 0.5 tsp salt, sugar, and baking powder.
3. Mix in the soy yoghurt and soy milk until a soft dough forms.
4. Knead the dough on a floured surface for a few minutes until smooth.
5. Divide the dough into equal portions and roll each into a ball.
6. Roll each ball into an oval shape about 0.5 cm thick.
7. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat and cook each naan for 2-3 minutes on each side until golden brown.
8. Brush each naan with the 2 garlic cloves, minced butter and sprinkle with chopped 0.5 cup fresh coriander, chopped before serving.

## Notes
- The flavour of this dish depends on cooking the masala properly rather than rushing straight to the final simmer.
- Taste before serving and adjust the salt, heat or acidity only once the dish has cooked fully.
- Leftovers often taste even better the next day once the spices have had more time to settle.
- Store leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days.
