## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 1 green chilli, finely chopped
- 2 tsp coriander powder
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- 200 g firm tofu, cubed
- 1 tsp salt
- mixed seasonal vegetables, chopped
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pan and add cumin seeds. Sauté until they sizzle.
2. Add chopped 1 large onion, finely chopped and green chilli, cooking until the onion is translucent.
3. Stir in minced 4 cloves garlic, minced and sauté for a couple of minutes until fragrant.
4. Add the mixed vegetables, turmeric, 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped powder, and 1 tsp salt. Cook for a few minutes, stirring occasionally.
5. Add tofu cubes and a splash of water if necessary. Cover the pan and let it simmer until the vegetables are tender.
6. Finish with 1 tbsp lemon juice and chopped 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped before serving.

## Notes
- The flavour of this dish depends on cooking the masala properly rather than rushing straight to the final simmer.
- Taste before serving and adjust the salt, heat or acidity only once the dish has cooked fully.
- Leftovers often taste even better the next day once the spices have had more time to settle.
- Store leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days.
