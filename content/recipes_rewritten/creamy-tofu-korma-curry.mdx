## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 green cardamom pods
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 1 medium tomato, chopped
- 200 g firm tofu, cubed
- 1 cup vegetable stock
- 1 cup water
- 1 tsp salt
- 50 g cashews
- pepper to taste
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tin coconut milk
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Toast cumin and 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped seeds in a dry pan until fragrant, then grind into a powder.
2. Blend 1 large onion, finely chopped, garlic, ginger, tomato, spices, and the ground seeds into a smooth curry paste.
3. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pan and fry cubed tofu until lightly golden. Set aside.
4. Add curry paste to the pan with coconut milk and vegetable stock. Simmer gently.
5. Blend cashews with water until smooth to make cashew cream.
6. Stir cashew cream into the sauce and simmer until thick and creamy.
7. Return tofu to the pan and warm through.
8. Finish with 1 tbsp lemon juice, seasoning, and 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped.

## Notes
- The flavour of this dish depends on cooking the masala properly rather than rushing straight to the final simmer.
- Taste before serving and adjust the salt, heat or acidity only once the dish has cooked fully.
- Leftovers often taste even better the next day once the spices have had more time to settle.
- Store leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days.
