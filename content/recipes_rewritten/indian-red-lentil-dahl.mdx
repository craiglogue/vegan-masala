## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp coconut oil
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 1-inch piece of ginger, grated
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
- 1 cup red lentils, rinsed
- 4 cups vegetable broth
- 1 tsp salt
- coconut sugar (optional)
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tin coconut milk
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Heat the coconut 2 tbsp coconut oil in a saucepan and sauté the onion until soft.
2. Add the 3 garlic cloves, minced and 1-inch piece of ginger, grated; cook until fragrant.
3. Stir in the turmeric, cumin, paprika, and garam masala. Cook briefly to release their aroma.
4. Add the rinsed lentils and vegetable broth. Bring to a simmer and cook for 8–10 minutes.
5. Stir in the coconut milk and chopped 2 medium tomatoes, chopped. Simmer until the lentils are soft.
6. Adjust seasoning with 1 tsp salt, pepper, coconut sugar, and 1 tbsp lemon juice.
7. Serve with rice and garnish with 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped.

## Notes
- The flavour of this dish depends on cooking the masala properly rather than rushing straight to the final simmer.
- Taste before serving and adjust the salt, heat or acidity only once the dish has cooked fully.
- Leftovers often taste even better the next day once the spices have had more time to settle.
- Store leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days.
