## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 green cardamom pods
- 1/4 tsp ground cloves
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 1 inch piece of ginger, grated
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
- 1 medium aubergine, chopped
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup water
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- a handful of curry leaves
- 1 tin coconut milk
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Heat oven to 240°C (220°C fan). Toss aubergine with 2 tbsp oil, 1 tsp salt and pepper.
2. Roast for 30 minutes until caramelised and soft.
3. Heat remaining 2 tbsp oil in a large pot. Add mustard seeds and let them pop.
4. Add curry leaves and cook briefly until fragrant.
5. Add 1 large onion, finely chopped and cook until golden brown.
6. Add 2 medium tomatoes, chopped, 4 cloves garlic, minced, and 1 inch piece of ginger, grated. Cook until fragrant.
7. Stir in all ground spices and cook into a thick paste.
8. Add water and the roasted aubergine. Simmer gently for 30 minutes.
9. Stir in coconut milk and adjust seasoning to taste.

## Notes
- The flavour of this dish depends on cooking the masala properly rather than rushing straight to the final simmer.
- Taste before serving and adjust the salt, heat or acidity only once the dish has cooked fully.
- Leftovers often taste even better the next day once the spices have had more time to settle.
- Store leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days.
