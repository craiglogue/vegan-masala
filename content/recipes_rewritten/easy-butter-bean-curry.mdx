## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- ¼ tsp cinnamon
- Pinch ground cloves
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- ¼ tsp turmeric
- ½ tsp garam masala
- ½ tbsp ground cumin
- ½ tbsp ground coriander
- 2 large tomatoes, chopped
- 4 cups cooked butter beans (or 2 cups dried, soaked)
- ½ tsp salt (plus more to taste)
- 2¼ cups water
- ¼ tsp cayenne
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- 1½–3 tbsp lemon juice

## Method
1. Heat 2 tbsp neutral oil in a large pan and cook onions until soft and lightly golden.
2. Add 3 cloves garlic, crushed and 1 inch ginger, grated; cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Add 2 large tomatoes, chopped and ½ tsp salt (plus more to taste); cook until softened and jammy.
4. Stir in all spices and cook for 2–3 minutes.
5. Add cooked butter beans and water; bring to a boil.
6. Simmer for 10–15 minutes until thickened.
7. Finish with 1½–3 tbsp lemon juice and adjust ½ tsp salt (plus more to taste) to taste.
8. Serve hot with basmati rice.

## Notes
- The flavour of this dish depends on cooking the masala properly rather than rushing straight to the final simmer.
- Taste before serving and adjust the salt, heat or acidity only once the dish has cooked fully.
- Leftovers often taste even better the next day once the spices have had more time to settle.
- Store leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days.
