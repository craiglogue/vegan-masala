## Ingredients
- 1/4 of a small yellow onion, coarsely chopped
- 2-3 cloves garlic, chopped
- 1-inch piece of fresh ginger, chopped
- 2-8 green chilies, chopped (2 for mild, 4-8 for spicy)
- 1 bunch cilantro, washed and cut into 1-2 inch sections, including the stems
- 1/2 cup fresh mint, coarsely chopped
- 1/2 cup vegan plain unsweetened yogurt*
- 1/2-1 tsp salt, to taste
- 2-4 tbsp fresh squeezed lemon or lime juice, to taste

## Method
1. In a food processor or blender, add 1/4 of a small yellow onion, 2-3 chopped cloves of garlic, 1-inch piece of chopped ginger, and 2-8 chopped green chilies. Process until finely chopped.
2. Next, incorporate 1 bunch of cilantro, 1/2 cup of coarsely chopped fresh mint, 1/2 cup of vegan yogurt, 1/2 tsp salt, and 2 tablespoons of lemon or lime juice. Blend until smooth, allowing for some texture.
3. Taste the chutney and adjust the seasonings, particularly the salt and lemon or lime juice, to enhance the flavors.

## Notes
- This chutney is best enjoyed fresh in the first week. Store it in an airtight container in the fridge, and consider freezing it in portions for longer storage.
- When reheating, allow it to come to room temperature for the best flavor. Serve it slightly chilled or at room temperature for optimal taste.
