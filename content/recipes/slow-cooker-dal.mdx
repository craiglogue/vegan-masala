## Ingredients
- 300g yellow split peas
- 1 onion, chopped
- 200g chopped tomatoes (from a tin or fresh)
- thumb of fresh root ginger, finely grated
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp ground turmeric
- 2 garlic cloves, one crushed and one thinly sliced
- 10 freeze-dried curry leaves
- 700ml hot vegetable stock
- 1 hot green finger chilli, thinly sliced
- 2 tbsp vegetable or sunflower oil
- lemon wedges, to serve

## Method
1. In a slow cooker, combine 300g yellow split peas, 1 chopped onion, 200g chopped tomatoes, thumb of grated ginger, 2 tsp ground cumin, 2 tsp ground turmeric, 1 crushed garlic clove, 10 freeze-dried curry leaves, and 700ml hot vegetable stock. Add most of the 1 thinly sliced green chilli and stir to combine.
2. Cook on high for four hours, until the split peas are tender. Season generously with salt and freshly ground black pepper.
3. In a saucepan, heat 2 tbsp oil until very hot. Add 1 tsp whole cumin seeds and the sliced garlic. Fry until the garlic turns golden-brown and the cumin releases a smoky aroma.
4. Spoon the hot spiced oil over the dal, scatter with the remaining chilli, and serve with lemon wedges for squeezing.

## Notes
- This dal can be stored in portion-sized containers in the freezer for up to 2 months. When reheating, add a splash of water if the dal thickens.
- For a smoother texture, blend the cooked dal before serving. This dish pairs beautifully with rice or crusty bread.
- Adjust the spice level by increasing or reducing the amount of green chilli. Feel free to garnish with fresh coriander for added freshness.
